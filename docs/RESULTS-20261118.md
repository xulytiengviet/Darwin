## Interpretation and scientific limits

1. **The experiment did not demonstrate a positive advantage** from CVNSS4.0-derived initialization over the unseeded control.
2. The CVNSS4.0 paired interval includes zero; no reliable improvement is established by these eight synthetic trials. The test also lacks externally calibrated effect sizes and multiple-comparison correction.
3. This benchmark evaluates optimization of a hidden artificial symbol target. It has no intrinsic Vietnamese language semantics; it is a **weak test of initialization**, not of speech-to-action, emergent language, genotype semantic inheritance, or self-growing LLM.
4. Different methods producing 24-symbol hashes may look like randomized initial populations. An independent causal benefit from the CVNSS4.0 rules has not been shown.
5. These initial results make a credible **negative pilot** and demonstrate traceability through code, deterministic seeds and CI artifacts. They do **not** constitute a Q1-ready conclusion.
6. Meaningful next experiment: make evolutionary selection evaluate **phenotype behavior** generated by `src/development.mjs`, then compare on new environments and ground-truth speech/communication tasks. This is needed before advancing beyond evidence level E2.
